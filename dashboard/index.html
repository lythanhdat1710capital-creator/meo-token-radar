How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains. Every input comes from the snapshot itself; no protocol name is ever special-cased.

  1. Category multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-per-TVL of the tokens already trading in that category, shrunk toward the market-wide median () with a prior of 8, clamped to 0.05–1.00. A category with few live tokens counts less; one with none takes the market-wide median.
  2. Growth. The seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, moves the value by 0.6× the change. Unknown growth counts as 0.
  3. Maturity. The value rises with age to a full 1.00 at two years, then eases to 0.80 by five years: longevity proves staying power, but five silent years suggest the team may never issue a token. Unknown age counts as one year.
  4. Chains. Each extra chain adds 2%, up to +20%: wider distribution, wider holder base.
